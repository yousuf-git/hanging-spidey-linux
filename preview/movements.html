Spidey movement preview

Behaviour mock-up on your real layout (two 1920×1080 monitors, top bar and left dock on monitor 1). The figure is a stand-in for the 3D Miles. He idles quietly, then makes one big move after a random delay. Drag him with the mouse anywhere (overview or close-up), or trigger moves from the panel.

What you're seeing
  • Idle: breathing, glancing around, slow sway on the line — no travel.
  • Big move: one at a time after a random wait (see “Next big move”).
  • Drag: he dangles from the cursor on a short web. Drop near a wall/ceiling → he sticks; in open space → he falls and lands.
  • Clickable area: only the dashed box around him blocks clicks; web, net and empty space pass clicks through.
  • Monitors: with roaming off he stays on one screen (the inner edge acts as a wall) until you drag him across or press “Send to other monitor”.
